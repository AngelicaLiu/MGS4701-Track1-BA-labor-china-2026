# Collection Protocol: Track 1 — BA Labor Market in China (2026)

## 1. Purpose
Documenting how the pilot dataset for Track 1 was collected.

## 2. Search terms
We used the following search terms: 商业分析, 数据分析, 经营分析.

## 3. Time window
Collection period: 2026-09-27 to 2026-09-28.

## 4. Sampling strategy
Convenience sample. Collectors rotated keywords to reduce ordering bias.

## 5. Inclusion criteria
1. Appeared on BOSS Zhipin public search pages.
2. Title or JD clearly related to BA/DA/BI/analytics.
3. Location was in one of China's first-tier cities or new first-tier cities.
4. At least title, company, city, and JD text were visible.
5. Not a duplicate.

## 6. Exclusion criteria
1. Pure sales, software engineering, or unrelated roles.
2. Outside mainland China.
3. Duplicates.

## 7. Coding rules
- Role family assigned based on job title, then JD text if ambiguous.
- AI-related skill flag = 1 if JD contains predefined AI keywords.
- Salary parsed into monthly RMB where possible. Missing salary coded as NA.
- City tier follows on common city-tier classifications used in China (e.g., 第一财经 2026 list).

## 8. Collection procedure
1. Search each keyword.
2. Collect postings according to inclusion/exclusion rules.
3. Copy fields into the coding sheet.
